# Unmet SMB / freelancer / solo-professional demand for paid "upload a file -> get a finished deliverable" AI services (as of Oct 2026)

Method note: 18 searches across vendor pricing pages, pricing guides, Capterra/G2 listings, Fiverr gig pages, IndiaMART, ICAI pages and industry forums (IFSQN, TaxTMI). Reddit threads did not show up in search results, so there are **no Reddit quotes** here (see Gaps). Most price figures come from vendor or agency pages. They are advertised rates, not audited market data.

Labels used: **OPEN GAP** = no credible product found that does upload-to-deliverable for this buyer · **WEAK COMPETITORS** = a few small or early tools, or tools that cover only part of the job · **CROWDED** = many products, or well-funded players.

---

## Q1. Which painful, repetitive document jobs do small businesses outsource or hate? (15 candidate ideas, each with demand evidence)

### Takeaway
The strongest demand signals are jobs that small firms **already pay humans per job** to turn documents into a compliance or bid deliverable: site-specific safety plans, HACCP plans, construction takeoffs, roofing insurance supplements, India GST notice replies, GeM/tender bid packs, and EU VSME/ESG supplier reports. In several of these, the human price ($150 to $5,000+ per job) is far above the cost of producing an AI first draft.

### Cited Findings
**1. Construction takeoff -> priced bid for small subcontractors (US/global)**
- There is no standard rate for outsourced estimating. A 2026 guide suggests budgeting $150-$500 for a small single-trade takeoff, $500-$2,500 for a detailed single-trade or small multi-trade estimate, and $2,500-$5,000+ for complex multi-trade work — [HireConstructionEstimator](https://hireconstructionestimator.com/blog/construction-estimating-services-cost)
- One provider prices single-trade/subcontractor takeoffs from $150 and multi-trade/GC takeoffs from $300. Its monthly plans start at $1,350 — [Construction Takeoff Services pricing](https://constructiontakeoffservices.com/pricing/)
- Several small estimating firms quote about $200 per takeoff, or $250 for any trade takeoff plus cost estimate — [Constructem](https://constructem.com/affordable-construction-takeoff-services-cost-pricing-models/); [World Estimating pricing](https://worldestimating.com/pricing/)
- A $300 takeoff and a $1,200 estimate are different products. The estimate adds labor, material pricing, waste, equipment, quote leveling, overhead, marked plans and a revision — [HireConstructionEstimator](https://hireconstructionestimator.com/blog/construction-estimating-services-cost)

**2. Site-Specific Safety Plan (SSSP) / OSHA safety program from project docs (US; similar needs in UK RAMS, Canada, AU)**
- A consultant charges $1,500-$5,000 for a written plan for one project and $800-$2,500 per day. Building a full OSHA-compliant safety program for a new company costs $8,000-$20,000 — [Greenberg Safety 2026 pricing guide](https://greenbergsafety.com/blog/construction-safety-consultant-cost)
- Generic templates cost about $20-$30 and are not customized. A NY SSSP template sells for $207.98 — [OHS Manuals](https://www.ohsmanuals.com/osha-site-specific-safety-plan-template-new-york.html); [XO Safety](https://www.xosafety.com/products/construction-safety-and-health-plan)
- A document-generation service charges $149 one-time for an OSHA program, plus a $199 toolkit or $298 for both. It recommends professional review for site-specific conditions — [CrewCompliance pricing](https://crewcompliance.org/pricing)
- A project SSSP takes 2-5 business days in-house, and a consultant takes about a week. GCs require one from subs before they mobilize — [Evolution Safety Resources](https://evolutionsafetyresources.com/site-specific-safety-plan-contents-what-to-include/)

**3. HACCP / food-safety plan for small food businesses (US, EU, UK, India FSSAI FoSTaC)**
- Consultants charge $1,500-$5,000 for HACCP work — [Xenia](https://www.xenia.team/articles/how-to-write-a-haccp-plan). RCP Food Safety lists $2,000 per HACCP plan — [RCP](http://www.rcpfoodsafety.com/fees--payment.html). Finley charges from $3,250 per plan plus $0-$600 in application fees — [Finley F&B](https://www.finleyfbconsulting.com/haccp)
- A 2026 guide puts a consultant-led plan at $2,500-$15,000 and hourly rates at $95-$400 in the US. HACCP software costs $24-$250 per month — [Audit-Binder](https://www.audit-binder.com/blog/haccp-plan-cost)
- An IFSQN forum thread treats $150-$400/hr as reasonable — [IFSQN](https://www.ifsqn.com/forum/index.php/topic/48846-haccp-plan-development-cost/)

**4. India GST notice -> drafted reply (India)**
- CA fees per reply run about ₹1,500-₹15,000+, or ₹3,000-₹10,000/month on retainer. These figures come from an AI vendor's comparison page — [GST Reply AI: AI vs CA](https://gstreplyai.com/gst-notice-reply-ai-vs-ca)
- Firms advertise ASMT-10 replies from ₹2,999 and show-cause notice replies from ₹5,999 — [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/gst-notice); [GSTwale](https://gstwale.in/services/gst/gst-notice-reply); [CallMyCA](https://callmyca.com/-gst-notice-reply)
- A practitioner forum says SCN reply drafting is usually billed outside the annual retainer — [TaxTMI forum](https://www.taxtmi.com/forum/issue?id=115885)

**5. India GeM / government tender bid pack (India)**
- One consultancy charges ₹15,000 for a basic package. Another charges ₹2,000 for GeM registration and ₹10,000 for 7 tender bids. Basic registration help starts at ₹2,000-₹5,000 — [LegalAdda](https://legaladda.com/gem-tender-bidding-consultant/); [VakilAdda](https://vakiladda.com/gem-registration-and-tender-consultant/); [GemTenderService](https://gemtenderservice.com/)
- Each bid needs the same document stack to be assembled and checked against the NIT: GST, PAN, Udyam, DSC Class III, 3 years of audited financials, solvency certificate, experience certificates and power of attorney. MSMEs also claim EMD exemption and turnover relaxation — [TenderFlowPro](https://tenderflowpro.in/blog/complete-government-tender-guide-india-2026); [IndianTenders](https://www.indiantenders.in/blogs/complete-guide-to-tenders-in-india-2c9/)

**6. US government (federal/SLED) proposal response for small contractors (US)**
- GovDash raised $30M for an AI GovCon platform that shreds an RFP into a compliance matrix and drafts against the evaluation criteria — [GovDash funding post](https://www.govdash.com/blog/govdash-raises-30m-in-new-funding); [GovDash](https://www.govdash.com/)
- AI-drafted federal proposals still need thorough human compliance review — [AI Pro Playbook](https://aiproplaybook.com/tools/govdash)

**7. Roofing / restoration insurance supplement from photos + carrier estimate (US)**
- Supplement firms charge 8-15% of the extra money recovered, or a flat $100-$150 per supplement — [Estimate on Demand](https://estimateondemand.com/estimate-supplement-pricing/). The Estimate Company charges 15% of supplemental recovery — [The Estimate Company](https://theestimatecompany.com/services/supplemental-estimating-service/)
- Xactimate roof estimates sell for $59 flat (EstimateWriters) and $50 (American Roof Supplements) — [EstimateWriters](https://estimatewriters.com/roof-estimators/); [American Roof Supplements](https://americanroofsupplements.com/roof-estimates/)

**8. Restaurant supplier invoices -> food cost / P&L (US first; global)**
- MarginEdge costs $350 per location per month — [MarginEdge pricing](https://www.marginedge.com/pricing/). Restaurants pay that much just to get line-item invoice capture and food costing.

**9. Prior authorization packets for small clinics (US)**
- According to the AMA, physicians and their staff spend about 13 hours a week on prior authorization — [Health AI Daily](https://healthaidaily.com/blog/best-ai-prior-authorization-software-small-practices-2026/) (secondary citation of the AMA)

**10. Product photos -> marketplace listings (Amazon global; Flipkart/Meesho India)**
- Fiverr Amazon listing gigs cost $10-$200. One optimization gig shows 693 reviews at 4.9 stars — [Fiverr listing-optimization category](https://www.fiverr.com/gigs/listing-optimization); [Fiverr gig $50](https://www.fiverr.com/vinctosc/do-a-great-amazon-fba-listing-optimization); [Fiverr gig $100](https://www.fiverr.com/lsp_writes/write-outstanding-amazon-listing-to-rank-your-product-with-seo)
- An Indian cataloging agency charges ₹1,000 + 18% GST per portal for up to 50 SKUs, then ₹20 + GST per product. Image editing costs extra — [IndiaMART listing](https://www.indiamart.com/proddetail/product-listing-service-on-amazon-flipkart-meesho-25496407488.html)

**11. Grant applications (US/UK/EU nonprofits and small businesses)**
- Demand is shown by the many paid tools in this space (see Q3). I found no independent figures for grant-writer fees.

**12. Commercial lease abstraction for small landlords / property managers (US mainly)**
- Full-service outsourced abstraction costs $150-$400 per lease. Offshore abstraction costs $5-$25 per lease and US firms charge $15-$50 — [Lextract](https://lextract.io/resources/articles/how-much-does-lease-abstraction-cost); [LeaseCodex](https://leasecodex.com/lease-abstraction-cost/)

**13. EU VSME sustainability report / supplier ESG questionnaire (EU, plus exporters to the EU)**
- EFRAG published VSME in Dec 2024. In July 2025 the Commission recommended that large companies limit their data requests to SME partners to what VSME contains — [Hedgehog](https://www.hhc.earth/knowledge-base/articles/vsme-standard)
- A consultant-led VSME project for an SME costs €8,000-€25,000 — [NGS Finland](https://ngsfinland.fi/en/articles/vsme-and-csrd-for-smes/). One US-focused vendor puts first-year costs at $28k-$55k — [Fiegenbaum](https://www.fiegenbaum.solutions/en/blog/vsme-sustainability-reporting-basic-vs-comprehensive-modules)
- EFRAG's own cost-benefit analysis finds VSME has a negative net impact in year one because of implementation costs — [EFRAG](https://www.efrag.org/system/files/sites/webpublishing/Project%20Documents/2309261112573240/EFRAG's%20Cover%20Letter%20and%20Cost%20Benefit%20Analysis%20on%20VSME.pdf)

**14. Bank statements -> Tally accounting entries (India CA firms / SMBs)**
- ICAI offers members a 50% discount on Suvit, a tool that turns bank-statement PDFs into Tally vouchers. This is a sign the professional body sees demand — [ICAI benefits](https://bs.icai.org/suvit-2/); [Suvit blog](https://www.suvit.io/post/auto-import-bank-statements-to-tally)

**15. Construction submittal packages from specs (US subs)**
- Tools exist to assemble submittal packages from specs and product data (see Q3). This is a repetitive document job that subcontractors have to do.

### Inferences
- The best ideas share three traits: (a) a human already charges per job, (b) the deliverable has a known template or regulation behind it (OSHA, HACCP/FSMA, CGST Act, GeM NIT, VSME), and (c) the buyer is too small for enterprise SaaS.
- Ideas 2, 3, 5 and 13 fit all three traits best. Ideas 7, 10, 11, 12 and 14 have proven demand but are already crowded.

### Gaps
- No Reddit, IndieHackers or HN threads came up in search. I could not quote first-hand complaints from r/Contractor, r/smallbusiness, r/AmazonSeller etc.
- Fiverr does not show order counts in search snippets. Review counts are the only proxy for volume.

## Q2. Who pays, what they pay a human today, how often, and market size

### Takeaway
Human prices range from about ₹3k (GST reply) through $150-$500 (single-trade takeoff) and $1.5k-$5k (SSSP/HACCP) to €8k-€25k (VSME). How often the job recurs differs a lot: SSSPs and takeoffs are needed per project or bid (high frequency), GST notices and tenders are episodic, and HACCP and VSME are annual or one-off with updates. I found almost no reliable market-size (TAM) figures.

### Inferences
- Price anchors for an AI-first product: about 10-30% of the human price per job. For example, an SSSP at $99-$299, a HACCP plan at $199-$499, a takeoff-to-bid at $49-$99, a GeM bid pack at ₹499-₹999, and a VSME report at €500-€2,000. Positioning against the human price rather than against SaaS seats looks feasible. This is inferred from the price gaps above, not tested.
- High-frequency ideas (takeoffs, SSSPs, GeM bids) suit monthly plans. Episodic ones (GST notice, HACCP, VSME) suit pay-per-job.

### Gaps
- I found no credible TAM figures for any of the niches. Counts of buyers (number of US subcontractors, Indian GeM sellers, EU SMEs in VSME scope, US food establishments needing HACCP) would need separate primary-source lookups (Census CBP, GeM dashboard, Eurostat).
- I found no published per-proposal fees for US federal proposal writers or SBIR writers, and GovDash pricing is not public ("contact for pricing") — [SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/govdash/pricing)
- I found no independent grant-writer fee data.
- EcoVadis fee figures conflict (€500-€3,000/yr vs $12k-$30k). The second figure probably refers to the buyer-side enterprise product, which I have not verified.

## Q3. Existing products, pricing, gaps, and a label for each idea

### Takeaway
Seven of the 15 ideas are crowded or held by well-funded players: grants, lease abstraction, roofing supplements, Amazon listings, prior auth, US GovCon proposals, and submittals. The clearest openings are compliance documents for micro-firms (SSSP, HACCP, VSME) and India-specific government paperwork (GeM bid packs, GST notice replies). In these, existing tools are templates, alert services, or one or two early AI entrants.

### Cited Findings
**2. SSSP / OSHA safety plans: WEAK COMPETITORS (close to an open gap)**
- The market today is templates ($20-$208), CrewCompliance's $149 generator (which says it is "not a safety consulting firm"), and consultants ($1.5k-$5k) — [CrewCompliance](https://crewcompliance.org/pricing); [Greenberg](https://greenbergsafety.com/blog/construction-safety-consultant-cost)
- Gap: I found no tool that reads the actual project documents (scope, drawings, GC requirements) and produces a GC-acceptable SSSP. GCs may also require credentials or specific formatting — [Greenberg](https://greenbergsafety.com/blog/construction-safety-consultant-cost)

**3. HACCP plans: WEAK COMPETITORS**
- Software costs $24-$250/month. FoodDocs starts at about $1,000/year. Most of these tools are monitoring/log apps rather than plan writers — [Audit-Binder](https://www.audit-binder.com/blog/haccp-plan-cost); [FoodDocs](https://www.fooddocs.com/post/food-safety-specialist)
- Gap: a menu/recipe/process upload that produces a regulator-ready plan with CCPs, aimed at single-location operators. Consultant prices of $1.5k+ suggest the price room is there.

**5. GeM / tender bid packs (India): OPEN GAP (unverified)**
- Search found only human consultancies (₹2k-₹15k packages) and tender-listing portals such as Tender18 and TendersInfo — [Tender18](https://tender18.com/gem-registration); [TendersInfo](https://www.tendersinfo.com/gem_registration)
- Gap: an AI that reads the NIT/bid document, checks eligibility against the MSME's uploaded certificates (Udyam, GST, financials, experience) and produces a compliant, ordered bid pack with a checklist.

**4. GST notice reply (India): WEAK COMPETITORS (early AI entrants)**
- GST Reply AI charges ₹299 per reply or ₹999/month unlimited for firms. It cites CGST sections and recommends CA review for high-value demands — [GST Reply AI](https://gstreplyai.com/)
- DigiLawyer.ai offers a GST notice reply drafting format — [DigiLawyer](https://digilawyer.ai/drafting/gst-notice-reply)
- Human services remain the norm. Complex or high-demand notices (SCN, hearings, >₹25 lakh) still need specialists — [Legal Suvidha](https://legalsuvidha.com/blog/gst-consultant-vs-ca-for-notice-response-who-should-you-hire/)
- Gap: a version sold to CA firms that also reconciles GSTR-1/3B/2B data against the notice. This is not verified as missing. Entrants already exist, so the window is narrowing.

**13. EU VSME / supplier ESG questionnaire: WEAK COMPETITORS (emerging)**
- Humboldt sells a VSME report "filled in from your data at a fixed price" — [Humboldt](https://humboldt.eco/frameworks/vsme)
- ExecutESG and CSRDPro offer VSME-to-EcoVadis mapping content and tools — [ExecutESG](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/vsme-ecovadis-preparation); [CSRDPro](https://www.csrdpro.com/en/vsme-report-improves-ecovadis-score/)
- EcoVadis has a free "Vitals" questionnaire that takes under an hour, for low-to-medium-risk suppliers. This may reduce demand for the lightest cases — [EcoVadis](https://ecovadis.com/solutions/esg-reporting-software/)
- Gap: a service that takes bills, invoices and HR exports and produces both the VSME report and pre-filled answers to customer questionnaires. It would sit between free Vitals and €8k+ consultants.

**1. Construction takeoff -> bid: CROWDED (software); WEAK for a done-for-you priced bid**
- Takeoff SaaS: Kreo from $35/mo, Easy Takeoffs $39/mo, Togal.AI from $299/user/mo (yearly), plus Beam AI, Handoff and STACK. No independent accuracy data exists. Beam's ±1% and Handoff's figures are vendor claims — [Dan Cumberland Labs](https://dancumberlandlabs.com/blog/ai-construction-estimating-software/); [Easy Takeoffs](https://easytakeoffs.com/blog/best-ai-takeoff-software)
- Gap: the tools produce quantities. Small subs still pay humans $500-$2,500 for a priced estimate with labor, waste and markup — [HireConstructionEstimator](https://hireconstructionestimator.com/blog/construction-estimating-services-cost). A trade-specific "plans in -> priced bid PDF out" service (e.g. drywall, painting, flooring) is the possible niche.

**8. Restaurant invoice -> food cost: CROWDED mid-market; WEAK at the single-location low end**
- MarginEdge $330-$350/location/mo; MarketMan from $199/user/mo; Cleo Pay $300-$330/location/mo (AP only); xtraCHEF Essentials needs manual coding of every purchase; Tab's Pro tier is $150/location/mo — [Capterra MarginEdge](https://www.capterra.com/p/187718/MarginEdge/); [Cleo Pay](https://www.cleo-pay.com/compare/marginedge); [MarginEdge vs xtraCHEF](https://www.marginedge.com/blog/marginedge-vs-xtrachef-which-one-is-right-for-independent-restaurant-operators); [Tab](https://tabcommerce.com/blog/restaurant365-alternatives)
- Gap: a pay-per-invoice or sub-$100/month service for a single independent restaurant or food truck. This is inferred from the price points; I found no complaints confirming it. Outside the US (India, EU) the main tools have little local presence (also an inference).

**7. Roofing insurance supplements: CROWDED (fast-filling)**
- AI entrants: RAFTER AI, RoofGenius, ESXPress (PDF -> Xactimate ESX), Chosen Local (AI plus human QA), Scooper AI, SupplementSnap — [RAFTER](https://rafterai.online/); [RoofGenius](https://roofgenius.ai/xactimate-supplement-software); [ESXPress](https://esxpress.org/tools/guides); [Chosen Local](https://chosen-local.com/supplements); [Scooper](https://www.scooperai.com/ai-automation-for-roofers); [Roofing Software Guide](https://roofingsoftwareguide.com/guides/how-to-write-roofing-supplements-with-software/)
- Possible remaining niche: restoration trades other than roofing, such as water/mold mitigation, interior and contents. Not verified.

**9. Prior authorization: CROWDED / FUNDED**
- CoverMyMeds and Tandem are free for providers. Honey Health charges about $1.50-$2 per PA. Others charge $5-$15 per PA or $500-$5,000/mo SaaS. Myndshft uses enterprise pricing. Insight Health uses outcome-based pricing — [Health AI Daily](https://healthaidaily.com/blog/best-ai-prior-authorization-software-small-practices-2026/); [Insight Health](https://www.insighthealth.ai/blog/top-ai-prior-authorization-software); [OmniMD](https://omnimd.com/blog/ai-prior-authorization-tools/)
- Sources conflict on per-PA pricing ($1.50-$2 vs $5-$15). Avoid this space unless targeting a narrow specialty, such as medical-benefit PAs in small specialty clinics.

**10. Marketplace listings: CROWDED (Amazon/English); WEAK for India multi-marketplace cataloging (unverified)**
- Fiverr Amazon listing gigs cost $10-$200 and are numerous — [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/gigs/amazon-listing-seo)
- In India, cataloging is still done by agencies at per-SKU rates (₹20/SKU) and image editing is extra — [IndiaMART](https://www.indiamart.com/proddetail/product-listing-service-on-amazon-flipkart-meesho-25496407488.html)
- Gap: product photo + bill -> ready-to-upload Flipkart/Meesho/Amazon.in bulk catalog files (category templates, HSN/GST, attributes). I found no product doing this in search, but I did not search further, so treat it as unverified.

**11. Grant applications: CROWDED**
- Grantable $24-$150/mo (with a 50% nonprofit discount), Instrumentl $299-$499/mo (drafting only in the $499 Pre-Award tier), Granted AI $19.99/mo, GrantCopilot $24/mo — [Grantable](https://grantable.co/guides/ai-grant-writing-tools); [AI Tools Bakery](https://aitoolsbakery.com/blog/grantable-vs-instrumentl/); [Granted AI](https://grantedai.com/blog/granted-ai-vs-instrumentl); [GrantCopilot](https://www.grantcopilot.ai/blog/best-grant-writing-software-2026)

**12. Lease abstraction: CROWDED at the low end**
- LeaseLens $25 per exported abstract; Lextract about $10 per lease with no subscription; enterprise platforms are sized for large portfolios — [LeaseCodex](https://leasecodex.com/lease-abstraction-cost/); [Lextract](https://lextract.io/resources/articles/how-much-does-lease-abstraction-cost)
- Possible niche: residential small landlords checking leases against local law (a rent-control or deposit-rule check). I did not research this.

**14. Bank statement -> Tally (India): CROWDED**
- Suvit handles PDF (including scanned or password-protected), Excel, CSV and XML statements, with rule-based ledger mapping and duplicate/GST checks, and it has ICAI backing — [Suvit](https://www.suvit.io/post/auto-import-bank-statements-to-tally); [ICAI](https://bs.icai.org/suvit-2/)
